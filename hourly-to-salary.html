Hourly to Salary Calculator

Turn an hourly rate into annual, monthly, weekly and daily income.

How it works

Annual pay = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year. Monthly pay is annual pay divided by 12. Lower the weeks per year to account for unpaid vacation.
