Activity Cleaner is installed

Your first cleanup takes about a minute. Nothing is deleted until you say so.

Open the demo full size

  1. Pin it to the toolbar. Click the puzzle icon at the top right of your browser, then the pin next to Activity Cleaner. Now it's one click away.
  2. Open LinkedIn and click the Activity Cleaner icon. Choose what to clean: Posts, Comments or Reactions. The extension opens the right activity page for you.
  3. Run a dry run first. “Dry run” is on by default. Press Start: it counts everything that would be removed and deletes nothing.
  4. Clean for real. Untick “Dry run” and press Start again. Keep the LinkedIn tab open. The extension works at a slow, human-like pace with short breaks, and you can press Stop at any moment.

Good to know
  • The free plan deletes up to 10 items a day. Pro removes the limit: $39/year, or a $9 pass for 7 days.
  • Your LinkedIn needs to be in English, Spanish, French or Dutch for now. You can switch it in LinkedIn: Settings → Account preferences → Language.
  • Deletion can’t be undone, so check the dry run count first.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Activity Cleaner never asks for your LinkedIn password.
