About BillableCalc

BillableCalc is a one-person project by Vitali Klachev, a QA automation engineer in Bulgaria. I build small, practical tools for freelancers and for people cleaning up their online presence, and I test them the way I test software for a living: with automated checks before every release.

What I make

  • Activity Cleaner for LinkedIn: a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox and Edge that deletes your own old LinkedIn posts, reposts, comments and likes, one at a time at a safe pace, with a dry run first.
  • BillableCalc Timer: a Chrome extension that reminds freelancers to start the timer when they open a client’s site and turns hours into an invoice.
  • Free calculators for freelance rates, project prices, invoices, late fees and taxes, plus plain-English guides.

How I build them

  • No accounts. You don’t sign up for anything. The calculators work in your browser.
  • Your data stays with you. The extensions never ask for your LinkedIn password, keep everything in your browser and have no servers that receive it. The only outside call is checking a Pro license key with Gumroad.
  • No tracking in the extensions. This website counts visits with cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics; see the privacy policy.
  • Honest pricing. Free plans that are actually usable, and Pro for people who need more. Payments go through Gumroad.

Why

LinkedIn has no “delete all” button, and most freelancers lose billable hours simply because they forget to press Start. Both are small, boring problems, which is exactly why nobody fixes them. I build in public and change things based on what users ask for: the “choose what to delete” mode and the timer’s snooze options both came from user feedback.
