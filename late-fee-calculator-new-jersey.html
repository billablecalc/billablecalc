New Jersey Late Fee Calculator for Unpaid Invoices

How much late interest to add to an overdue invoice in New Jersey. Pick the rate that applies, enter the days past due, and get the amount to put on your reminder.

In short: No agreed rate: 6% a year. Written agreement: up to 16% a year.

New Jersey late fee rules for invoices

Legal rate: 6% a year

New Jersey’s legal rate of interest is 6% a year.

Source: N.J.S.A. 31:1-1

Written agreements: up to 16% a year

A written contract can set a rate of up to 16% a year; loans over $50,000 are among the exceptions. Criminal usury starts at 30% a year for individuals and 50% for corporations and LLCs. A creditor who charges a usurious rate can lose the right to any interest.

Source: N.J.S.A. 31:1-1, 2C:21-19

Example

A $2,000 invoice, 45 days past due, at the 6% legal rate: 2,000 × 6% × 45 / 365 = $14.79 late interest, $2,014.79 total.

Looking for rent late fees? Residential leases follow New Jersey’s landlord-tenant rules, not the rules on this page.

How the calculator works

A monthly rate is applied as amount × rate × days / 30, a yearly rate as amount × rate × days / 365 (simple interest, no compounding). Add a flat fee only if your contract names one.

Questions

What is the maximum interest on a late invoice in New Jersey?

Up to 16% a year if agreed in writing; 6% a year if no rate was agreed.

Can I charge 1.5% a month in New Jersey?

1.5% a month is 18% a year, above the 16% civil limit for written contracts, so use 1.33% a month or less unless an exception applies.
