In short: No agreed rate: 8% a year. Otherwise the rate in your written contract.

Indiana late fee rules for invoices

No rate agreed: 8% a year

When the parties have not agreed a rate, Indiana sets interest on money owed at 8% per year.

Source: Ind. Code § 24-4.6-1-102

After a court judgment

Once you win a judgment, interest runs at your contract rate but no more than 8% a year, or 8% if there was no contract rate.

Source: Ind. Code § 24-4.6-1-101

Contract rates

A late fee or interest rate agreed in writing before work starts normally governs business invoices. Consumer credit has separate Indiana rules.

Example

A $2,500 invoice, 30 days late, no rate agreed: 2,500 × 8% × 30 / 365 = $16.44.

How the calculator works

A monthly rate is applied as amount × rate × days / 30, a yearly rate as amount × rate × days / 365 (simple interest, no compounding). Add a flat fee only if your contract names one.

Questions

What is the legal interest rate in Indiana?

8% a year when the parties have not agreed a rate.

Is post-judgment interest in Indiana capped?

Yes. After a judgment, interest runs at the contract rate but not more than 8% a year, or 8% without a contract rate.

  • Ind. Code § 24-4.6-1-101 (FindLaw)
