Privacy Policy — Activity Cleaner for LinkedIn

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy covers the Chrome extension Activity Cleaner for LinkedIn (“the extension”), made by BillableCalc.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension runs entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store or send your LinkedIn data, your browsing history or your login details to us or to any third party. We have no servers that receive data from the extension.

What is stored on your device

Your settings, a counter of how many items you cleaned today, the address of your LinkedIn profile (so the extension can open your activity pages), and your Pro license key if you bought one. This data is kept in Chrome’s local extension storage on your computer and never leaves it, except for the license check below. Removing the extension deletes it.

License check

If you activate Pro, the extension sends your license key to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to confirm the purchase, and re-checks it every few days. Gumroad’s privacy policy applies to that request. Payments are handled by Gumroad; we never see your card details.

Permissions

  • linkedin.com — to find and click the delete / remove buttons on activity pages that you open yourself.
  • api.gumroad.com — only for the license check.
  • storage — to keep your settings and daily counter on your device.

Selling or sharing data

We do not sell, rent or share any user data. Data is not used for advertising, credit-worthiness or any purpose unrelated to the extension’s single purpose: cleaning your own LinkedIn activity.

Not affiliated with LinkedIn

The extension is an independent tool. LinkedIn is a trademark of LinkedIn Corporation.
