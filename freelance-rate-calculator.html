Freelance Hourly Rate Calculator

Find the rate you must charge to reach your target income after expenses, taxes and unpaid time.

Why billable hours matter

Freelancers spend much of their time on admin and sales, so billable hours are usually 20-30 per week, not 40. This calculator grosses up your income goal for taxes and expenses, then divides by the hours you can actually bill.
