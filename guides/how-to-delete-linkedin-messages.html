How to Delete LinkedIn Messages

Short answer: you can delete a message for everyone only within 60 minutes of sending it: hover it, click More (…) → Delete. After that you can only delete the whole conversation from your inbox. The other person keeps their copy.

Unsend a message (within 60 minutes)

  1. Open Messaging and the conversation.
  2. Hover over your message (in the app, press and hold it) and click More (…).
  3. Choose Delete and confirm.

The message disappears for everyone in the conversation and is replaced with “This message has been deleted”. In the same 60 minutes you can also choose Edit to fix a typo; the message then shows an “Edited” label.

InMail works differently: once it’s sent, it can’t be deleted.

Delete a whole conversation

Desktop:

  1. Open Messaging.
  2. Hover over the conversation in the list and click the three dots.
  3. Choose Delete conversation and confirm.

App: open the conversation, tap the three dots at the top right, choose Delete conversation and confirm.

This is permanent for you, but it only removes the conversation from your inbox. The other person still has every message. Deleting a conversation doesn’t remove the connection either.

Can I delete all LinkedIn messages at once?

LinkedIn has no “delete all” option for messages. You delete conversations one by one. If you want your messages gone everywhere for good, closing your account is the only built-in way, and even then messages you sent stay in other people’s inboxes. See how to delete your LinkedIn account.

Cleaning up your public activity too?

Messages are private. Your posts, comments and likes are not: anyone can scroll them on your profile. Delete old ones by hand from Profile → Activity → Show all, or in bulk with Activity Cleaner for LinkedIn, a free browser extension that runs a dry run first and deletes at a slow, safe pace. Get it from the Chrome Web Store.

FAQ

Can I unsend a LinkedIn message after an hour?

No. Deleting a message for everyone only works within 60 minutes of sending it. After that you can delete the conversation from your own inbox, but the other person keeps their copy.

Does the other person know I deleted a message?

Yes. A message deleted within 60 minutes is replaced with “This message has been deleted”, so they can see that something was removed.

If I delete a conversation, is it deleted for the other person?

No. Deleting a conversation only removes it from your inbox. The other person still sees all messages.

Can I delete an InMail I sent?

No. Once an InMail is sent, it can't be deleted.

Is there a way to delete all LinkedIn messages at once?

No. LinkedIn has no bulk delete for messages; conversations are deleted one by one.
