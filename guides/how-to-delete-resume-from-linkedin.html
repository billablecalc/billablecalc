How to Delete a Resume from LinkedIn

Short answer: go to Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Job application settings, click the three dots next to the resume and choose Delete. If you also added a resume to your profile, remove it from the Featured section.

Where LinkedIn keeps your resumes

  • Job application settings: resumes you uploaded when applying with Easy Apply. LinkedIn keeps your most recent ones so you can reuse them. Only you can see this list.
  • Featured section: a resume you added to your profile yourself. This one is public, so check it first.
  • Posts: if you ever shared your resume as a document post, it stays in your Activity until you delete the post.

Delete a saved resume

  1. Click Me → Settings & Privacy.
  2. Open Data privacy and, under job seeking preferences, click Job application settings.
  3. Click the three dots next to the resume.
  4. Choose Delete and confirm.

On the same page you can switch off saving uploaded resumes, so LinkedIn stops keeping new ones.

Remove a resume from your Featured section

  1. Click Me → View profile and scroll to Featured.
  2. Click the pencil icon.
  3. Click the three dots or the trash icon on the resume and choose Delete.

Remove a resume you shared in a post

Open Profile → Activity → Show all → Posts, find the post, click the three dots and choose Delete. If you posted a lot during a job search (“open to new roles”, “here is my CV”), Activity Cleaner for LinkedIn can remove old posts, comments and likes in bulk: it runs in your browser, does a dry run first and deletes at a slow pace.

FAQ

Does deleting my resume withdraw my job applications?

No. Deleting a saved resume does not change applications you already sent. The employer keeps the copy they received.

Can other people see the resumes saved in my job application settings?

No. That list is private. Only a resume you add to your Featured section or share in a post is visible to others.

How do I stop LinkedIn saving my resumes?

In Settings & Privacy, then Data privacy, then Job application settings, switch off the option to save uploaded resumes.

Can I delete a resume in the LinkedIn app?

Yes. In the app, open Settings, then Data privacy, then Job application settings, and delete it there. Labels can differ slightly from desktop.
