How to Delete a Repost on LinkedIn

Short answer: open your Activity, find the card that says “your name reposted this”, click the three dots on that card and choose Delete repost. If you only see Save or Report, you are on the original post, not on your repost.

Delete a repost (desktop)

  1. Click Me → View profile, scroll to Activity and click Show all.
  2. Find the card with “your name reposted this” at the top.
  3. Click the three dots at the top right of that card.
  4. Choose Delete repost.

Only your share goes away. The original post stays on the author’s profile with all its likes and comments.

App: open your profile, tap Show all activity, find the repost and tap the three dots on it → Delete repost.

If I delete a repost, will they know?

No. LinkedIn does not notify the original author or anyone who saw your repost. The repost disappears from your Activity and from your network’s feeds, and the repost count on the original post goes down by one. That counter is the only thing that could give it away. More on what people can and can’t see: If I delete a LinkedIn post, comment or repost, will they know?

Why it won’t delete

  • You opened the original post. Its menu belongs to the author, so there is no delete option for you. Go back to your Activity and use the menu on the repost card.
  • You added your own text. A repost with your thoughts is your own post. Its menu says Delete post, not Delete repost.
  • Someone else reposted you. Only they can delete their repost. You can delete your original post.

Delete many reposts at once

LinkedIn has no “delete all reposts” button: you remove them one by one. To do it in bulk, Activity Cleaner for LinkedIn works on your Activity page: turn on Also undo my reposts and run a dry run first to see how many it finds. With Pro you can also tick exactly which reposts, posts or comments to remove. Get it from the Chrome Web Store (also on Firefox and Edge).

FAQ

If I delete a repost on LinkedIn, will they know?

No. The original author and your network are not notified. Only the repost count on the original post goes down by one.

Why don't I see “Delete repost”?

You are probably looking at the original post. Its three-dot menu belongs to the author and only offers options like Save or Report. Open your own Activity, find the card that says “<your name> reposted this” and use the three dots on that card.

Does deleting my repost delete the original post?

No. Delete repost removes only your share. The original stays on the author’s profile with its likes and comments.

How do I delete a repost I added my own text to?

A repost with your thoughts is a post of yours. Open its three-dot menu and choose Delete post.

Can I remove someone else’s repost of my post?

No. Only the person who reposted can delete their repost. You can delete your own original post.

Can I delete all my reposts at once?

LinkedIn has no bulk option: reposts are deleted one by one. A browser extension such as Activity Cleaner can undo them in bulk from your Activity page.
