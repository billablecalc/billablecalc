How to Delete All Your Facebook Comments and Likes at Once

Updated November 2026

Short answer: your posts can be deleted in bulk with Facebook’s own Manage posts tool. Your comments, likes and reactions can’t: Facebook only lets you remove them one by one in Activity log. Here is where to find each of them and how to get through them faster.

WhatBulk delete built into Facebook?Where
PostsYesProfile → Manage posts
CommentsNo, one by oneActivity log → Comments
Likes and reactionsNo, one by oneActivity log → Likes and reactions

Before you start: download a copy

Deleted comments and likes can’t be restored. If you might want them later, download your data first: Settings & privacy → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.

Delete all your Facebook posts at once

  1. Open your profile and click Manage posts (on some accounts it’s under the … menu next to Edit profile).
  2. Use Filters to pick a year or post type, then tick the posts you want, or Select all.
  3. Click Next and choose Move to trash or Archive (only you can see archived posts).

Posts stay in trash for 30 days before Facebook deletes them for good, so you can still change your mind.

Delete your Facebook comments

  1. Click your profile picture → Settings & privacy → Activity log.
  2. In the left menu open Your activity across Facebook → Interactions → Comments. Use the date filter to jump to a year.
  3. Click the … next to a comment → Delete → confirm.

Older accounts sometimes still see checkboxes and a Select all option here; most accounts in 2026 only get the menu next to each comment. On the app the path is Menu → Settings & privacy → Activity log → Comments, also one at a time.

Remove your Facebook likes and reactions

  1. In Activity log, open Interactions → Likes and reactions.
  2. Click the … next to an item → Unlike or Remove reaction.

There’s no bulk unlike. If you have years of likes, set aside a few short sessions and work back through the dates.

What about bulk tools?

A few browser extensions click through Activity log for you. Before using one, know that Meta’s terms restrict automated tools, and accounts that act too fast can be temporarily blocked. If you use one, pick a tool that works slowly, runs in your own browser, never asks for your password and lets you test first without deleting anything.

Tips

  • Nobody is notified. Deleting a comment or removing a like sends no alert; the counts on the post just go down.
  • Replies go too. Deleting your comment also removes the replies under it.
  • Hide instead of delete. If you only want old posts off your profile, Archive keeps them private instead of deleting them.

FAQ

Can I delete all my Facebook comments at once?

Not with one button. In the current Activity log you delete comments one at a time from the menu next to each one. Some older accounts still see checkboxes to select several at once.

Can I remove all my likes on Facebook at once?

No. Facebook has no bulk unlike. You remove likes and reactions one by one in Activity log, under Likes and reactions.

Can I delete all my Facebook posts at once?

Yes. Facebook's Manage posts tool lets you select many of your own posts and move them to trash or archive in one go.

Does Facebook notify anyone when I delete a comment?

No. The comment simply disappears from the post and the comment count goes down.
