Redact vs Unpost vs Activity Cleaner: Best Way to Bulk Delete LinkedIn Posts

Short answer: LinkedIn has no “delete all” button, so you need a tool. Redact has the most filters but LinkedIn needs its paid Ultimate plan. Unpost is a phone app with the most generous free tier. Activity Cleaner is a Chrome extension that works in the LinkedIn tab you already have open, never asks you to log in again, and costs $9 once. Pick by where you want to work and how much filtering you need.

Disclosure: we make Activity Cleaner. We tried to keep this comparison fair; facts about the other tools come from their own websites as of October 2026, so check them for current prices.

Which one to choose

  • Choose Redact if you want to clean many platforms at once or need fine filters, like deleting only posts with a certain word or from a certain year, and you don’t mind a monthly subscription.
  • Choose Unpost if you prefer working from your phone, also want to clean other social networks, and want a bigger free daily limit.
  • Choose Activity Cleaner if you work on a computer in Chrome, want a one-time price for a one-time cleanup, and would rather not sign in to LinkedIn inside another app.

Safety tips for any tool

  • Preview first. Deletion on LinkedIn is permanent; check what will be removed before you start.
  • Go slowly. Hundreds of deletions in a few minutes can look like a bot. Tools that pause between actions are safer for your account.
  • Know the risk. LinkedIn’s User Agreement restricts automated tools, so any of these is used at your own risk.

FAQ

Is there a free way to bulk delete LinkedIn posts?

Unpost and Activity Cleaner both have free tiers with daily limits (50 and 10 deletions a day). Redact’s free plan doesn’t cover LinkedIn. Deleting by hand is always free, just slow.

Does LinkedIn notify anyone when I bulk delete?

No. LinkedIn doesn’t notify anyone when you delete a post, comment or like, no matter which tool you use.
