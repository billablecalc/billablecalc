How to Remove Open to Work on LinkedIn

Short answer: to remove only the green frame, click your profile photo → Frames → Original → Save. To turn Open to Work off completely, click the pencil on the Open to Work box on your profile and choose Delete. LinkedIn changes button labels from time to time, so look for the closest match.

Frame only, or the whole thing?

Open to Work has two visibility settings:

  • All LinkedIn members adds the public green #OpenToWork frame to your photo. Anyone who visits your profile sees it.
  • Recruiters only shows your job preferences only to people using LinkedIn Recruiter. There is no frame. LinkedIn says it tries to hide this from recruiters at your current company but can’t guarantee it.

Many people keep Open to Work on for recruiters and just drop the frame. Others turn it off once they have a new job.

Remove only the green frame (desktop)

  1. Go to your profile and click your profile photo.
  2. Choose Frames.
  3. Select Original (no frame) and click Save.

Your job preferences stay on and switch to recruiters only.

Turn Open to Work off completely (desktop)

  1. Click Me → View profile.
  2. In the Open to work box under your name, click the pencil icon.
  3. Scroll down and click Delete (shown as “Delete from profile” or “Delete job preference”).
  4. Confirm.

In the LinkedIn app

Tap your profile photo → View profile, then tap the Open to work box and use the edit option to delete it. The labels in the app are close to the desktop ones.

Starting a new job? Tidy up the rest too

The frame is the most visible sign of a job search, but not the only one. Old posts and comments asking for referrals, “I’m looking for my next role” updates and likes on job-hunting content stay in your Activity section. Delete the ones you no longer want by hand from Profile → Activity → Show all, or in bulk with Activity Cleaner for LinkedIn, a free browser extension that runs a dry run first and deletes at a slow, safe pace. FAQ

Does LinkedIn notify my network when I remove Open to Work?

No. Removing the frame or deleting your job preferences does not send a notification or create a post.

Can my current employer see that I'm open to work?

With “Recruiters only” there is no frame, and LinkedIn tries to hide the signal from recruiters at your company, but it can't guarantee it. With “All LinkedIn members” anyone, including your employer, sees the green frame.

Will removing the frame turn off Open to Work?

No. Switching your photo frame to Original only removes the frame. Your preferences stay visible to recruiters until you delete them from the Open to work box.

What about my old #OpenToWork post?

If you announced your search in a post, it stays in your Activity until you delete it. Open the post, click the three dots and choose Delete.
