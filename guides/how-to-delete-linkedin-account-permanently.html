How to Delete Your LinkedIn Account Permanently

Short answer: click Me → Settings & Privacy → Account preferences → Account management → Close account, pick a reason, enter your password and confirm. You have 14 days to change your mind. After that the account is gone for good.

Before you close it

  • Download your data. Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data. The archive usually arrives by email within a day. It includes your connections, messages and posts.
  • Cancel Premium. An active Premium subscription blocks closing. If you pay through the App Store or Google Play, cancel it there. Deleting the app doesn’t stop billing.
  • Hand over groups and Company Pages. Transfer group ownership and add another admin to any Company Page you manage.
  • Save your recommendations. They are not restored even if you reactivate.

Close your account on desktop

  1. Click Me → Settings & Privacy.
  2. Open Account preferences and scroll to Account management.
  3. Click Close account, choose a reason and click Next.
  4. Enter your password and click Close account.

Close your account in the app

  1. Tap your profile photo → Settings → Account preferences.
  2. Under Account management, tap Close account.
  3. Pick a reason, enter your password and confirm.

If you don’t see the option, update the app or use linkedin.com in a browser.

What happens after you close it

  • Right away: your profile disappears from LinkedIn and you are signed out everywhere.
  • Within 14 days: sign in with the same email and password and choose Reactivate. Most of your profile and connections come back. Endorsements, recommendations, group memberships, follows and pending invitations do not.
  • After 14 days: the closure is permanent. Messages you sent stay in other people’s inboxes, usually shown as from “A LinkedIn member”.
  • Google: your profile can still show in search results for a few weeks until search engines refresh.

Not sure? Two alternatives

Hibernate. Account preferences → Account management → Hibernate account hides your profile but keeps your connections, messages and settings. Your past posts show as from “A LinkedIn member”. You can come back later.

Clean up instead of deleting. If what bothers you is old content, not the account itself, delete that content and keep your network. Old posts, comments and likes can be removed by hand from Profile → Activity → Show all, or in bulk with Activity Cleaner for LinkedIn, a free browser extension that runs in your browser, does a dry run first and never asks for your password. FAQ

How long does LinkedIn keep my account after I close it?

You can reactivate within 14 days. After that the closure is permanent and the account can't be recovered.

Why can't I close my LinkedIn account?

The usual reasons are an active Premium subscription or owning a group. Cancel Premium (including in the App Store or Google Play) and transfer group ownership, then try again in a browser.

Does closing my account delete my messages?

Your copy is deleted, but messages you sent stay in the other person's inbox, usually shown as from a LinkedIn member.

What is the difference between closing and hibernating?

Closing deletes the account after 14 days. Hibernating hides your profile but keeps your connections, messages and settings so you can come back later.

Can I delete my old posts without closing my account?

Yes. Delete them one by one from your Activity section, or use a bulk tool such as Activity Cleaner for LinkedIn.
