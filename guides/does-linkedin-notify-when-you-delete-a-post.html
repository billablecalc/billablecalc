Does LinkedIn Notify Anyone When You Delete a Post?

Short answer: no. LinkedIn does not notify your connections, your followers or the people who liked or commented when you delete a post. The same goes for deleting a comment or removing a like: nobody gets an alert. The content simply disappears.

What happens when you delete a post

  • It disappears everywhere on LinkedIn: from your profile’s Activity, from feeds and from LinkedIn search.
  • Its likes and comments go with it. All reactions and comments on the post are removed together with the post.
  • It cannot be restored. LinkedIn has no trash or undo for posts. Copy the text and images first if you might want them later.
  • Notifications that were already sent stay sent. If you tagged someone, they were notified when you published. Deleting the post does not “un-send” that, but the link will no longer open the post.
  • Reposts of it stop showing your content. When someone reposted your post, the original no longer appears through their repost after you delete it.

What about comments and likes?

  • Deleting a comment removes it from the post along with any replies under it. The post author is not notified.
  • Removing a like (or Celebrate, Support and other reactions) just removes it from the post’s reaction count. Nobody is notified.

Will people notice?

Only if they go looking. Someone who remembers your post and opens your profile will see it is gone, and the author of a post may notice a comment or like disappeared from it. But there is no message, email or alert telling anyone that you deleted something.

FAQ

Does LinkedIn notify people if I edit a post instead?

No. Editing a post does not send a new notification. The post shows an “Edited” label.

Does deleting old posts hurt my profile or reach?

LinkedIn has no known penalty for deleting your own posts. You lose the likes and comments those posts collected, but your profile is not punished for cleaning up.

Can I recover a deleted LinkedIn post?

No. Deleted posts cannot be restored, so save anything you want to keep before you delete it.
